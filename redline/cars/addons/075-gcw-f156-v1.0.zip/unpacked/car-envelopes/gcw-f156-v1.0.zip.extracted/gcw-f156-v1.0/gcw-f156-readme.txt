Description:
An interpretation of a 1961 Ferrari 156 'Sharknose'.

It was Ferrari's first rear-engined Formula One car and was notable for it's extremely light weight and consequently massive power-to-weight ratio. This gives it a considerable advantage over the Cooper Climax, but the light weight and skinny tyres mean that grip is hard to come by.

This is the car that Phil Hill and Wolfgang 'Taffy' von Trips drove in the 1961 Formula One championship. Phil Hill went on to win the championship but sadly Von Trips and fourteen spectators were killed when his car left the track at Monza during the Italian Grand Prix.

The bodywork was redesigned in 1963 losing the distinctive 'sharknose' but still scoring wins for John Surtees and Lorenzo Bandini. Then all of the cars were scrapped.

The only existing 156 is a replica built for Chris Rea.

I based this model on photos and models of the car, particularly the features of the car during Phil Hill's record-breaking under nine minute run at the Nurburgring Nordschleife.

The model is quite detailed, with 9776 triangles in total including the wheels and driver.
