File:
Toyota MR2 Spyder.redplug v1

Description:
A 2005 Toyota MR2 Spyder. This car has a V6 mid-engine and is rear-wheel drive. It is lighter than the Mini, but also has less power. It's gearing allows a higher top speed than the Mini, but it is slower off the line. The result is an interesting competitor to the Mini, Golf, and Audi TT.

Bugs:
The body doesn't cast a shadow. Also, I haven't learned how to do UV texture mapping yet so the car looks a bit "flat".
