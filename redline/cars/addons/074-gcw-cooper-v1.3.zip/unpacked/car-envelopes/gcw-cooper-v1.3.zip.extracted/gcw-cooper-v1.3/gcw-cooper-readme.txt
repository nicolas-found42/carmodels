Description:
An interpretation of a 1960 Cooper Climax T53 Formula One car.

The Cooper Climax, while not being the first rear-engined F1 car, marked the end of the line for forward-engined F1 cars. Designed by jack Brabham and powered by a 240HP Cooper Climax engine originally designed for a fire-pump, this funny little car powered to a number of Grand Prix victories in 1960.

I've included five colour-schemes from the period. 

Version 1.3:
D'oh!
I realised I made the wheels the diameter of the proper cooper's /wheel-rims/ (!) So I've added custom wheels of the right diameter to this model. The handling has had to be adjusted somewhat to accommodate this change.

I was  asked to add a brake light as the lack of one was causing racing accidents - I had to shove the racing number plate to one side, but it's there now. I also added one new colour scheme and version/badge art.

Version 1.2:
Road holding is improved by adding grippy racing tyres. This now drives as well as I'd expect a Formula One car to.
The driver model has been replaced with five new driver models, who no longer run their feet along the ground.
The engine friction has been adjusted for its slightly smaller size.

Version 1.1:
This adds a sample of a Climax engine in full spate and corrects many of the handling problems by shifting the CoG slightly and strongly stiffening the front suspension.

Bugs:
The driver model still sticks his hand out of the car to change gear.
