Description:
An interpretation of a 1935 Alfa Romeo Tipo C as driven by Tazio Nuvolari in grands prix of 1935 and 1936.

This Grand Prix racer has a 4 litre, 8 cylinder inline, engine with two roots superchargers generating an astonishing 330 bhp capable of driving this lightweight streamlined chassis all the way up to 175 mph. 8 were built before Alfa needed to move up to a 12 cylinder engine in the quest for greater and greater horsepower. The 8 cars mostly went to South America to compete in Formula Libre races.

Two survive today, one in the US and one which is being rebuilt/restored in New Zealand.

I've included seven colour schemes, but only the first (red one) can be considered as representing the real car. Five others are based on national racing colours and the seventh is a tribute to a great cartoon and the support of Leoantix at legacyoflies.com
