Description:
An interpretation of a 1933 Morgan three wheeler Aero Super Sports with a J.A.P. JTOR 996cc V-Twin racing engine.

Bugs:
I really wanted to include a nice .wav of a vtwin engine blapprrring away - but couldn't get Redline to accept it. Goshdarnit.

v1.01:
Corrected the handling somewhat - the wheels were unbalanced and too heavy.
Fixed the engine and green model graphics.

v1.10:
Now has the proper v-twin engine sound, a much revised model with proper shadowing, more skins, revised handling, drastically altered brakes and the proper name.

You're well advised to use the front hand-brakes entering a sharp deceleration rather than the rear wheel brakes, they provide a much more stable braking effect.
