Clio Sport 172 v1.0

August 2007 - Fixed, plates and missing line on .car file

November 2006

This model is about 700 polys so it should run smoothly.
Included 4 colors as sold in Mexico from 2002 to 2005.

I didn't model the interior 'cause I liked the way the windows looked.
I have some problems with environmental mapping from model smoothing and it looks a bit weird, but not too bad, I thought it was better to keep it low resolution and weird-reflections than high and smooth.

I tried to make it fun and real. It's supposed to go from 0-100km/h in about 7.2 seconds; it has traction control and I decided to cheat a bit to simulate this giving it a bit of traction on rear tires.

Try it at moonlight, I worked on lights quite a bit.

Hope you enjoy it!
