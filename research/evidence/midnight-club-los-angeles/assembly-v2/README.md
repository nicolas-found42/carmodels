# Native assembly and S7 preview cleanup evidence

This evidence records the `mcla-xbox360-static-stock-v2` native-frame upgrade and
the user-authorized `s7-source-pinned-divider-thinning-v1` preview reconstruction.
The native game resources and downloads are unchanged. All current previews use UInt32 triangle indices.

- `checks/` preserves the complete final affected-check manifest and logs. The earlier full local
  run had 96 passes and one Vantage exhaust-namespace failure; its manifest and failing log remain
  in `earlier-checks/`. The source-qualified anchor fix and regression resolve that failure.
- `native/` contains the all-resource calibration, independent source-byte reconstruction and
  full native decoder result. These establish source algebra, not execution of the original game.
- `s7/` contains the accepted helper reading, independent affine replay, source-corpus comparison,
  emitted-attribute proof and six corruption controls. The 105 other GLBs are byte-identical to
  the pre-cleanup v2 edition; only S7 skirt model 0, primitive 1 positions/normals change.
- `browser/` contains fresh Jev Browser manifests, exact independently checked load/image/hash
  coverage, metrics, visual observations and contact sheets for all 106 entries in each page.
  The original PNGs remain local at the recorded paths; the committed sheets are reduced audit views.
- `semantic/` records the 56 live TypeSafe filter requests, two wordings over 28 labeled states,
  provider usage/cost/latency, the production cross-catalog guard and exact final-state equivalence.
  The sample is not a general accuracy estimate.
- `gates/` retains final scoped Jev gates and independent resolutions. Scores, review statuses and
  counterevidence are preserved. Generated GLBs are checked by executable replay and hash assertions.

The S7 reading deliberately narrows the ten dividers to about 10% of native surface area, with
up to 93.437 mm movement. Their vertical spans, indices, coordinate seams and identities remain.
Small endpoints and native intake-cavity surfaces remain. Crossing-pair count is 144 after the
repair versus 114 before; summed finite intersection length falls from 2.437 m to 0.438 m.
This is a documented preview reconstruction, not zero intersections or recovered game deformation.

Two orbital views per entry establish bounded observed placement. Hidden faces, runtime factory
configuration, damage/animation and original glass/paint/reflection shader fidelity remain unverified.
Ambient Eclipse roof-light, service-van storage and garbage-truck lifting poses retain their native
appearance/pose qualifications. Five semitrailer packages are intentionally cabless.

`artifact-pins.json` hashes the saved evidence files. Original failed or superseded judgments are
retained separately from accepted completion claims; an independent resolution never approves a
contradicted claim or an operational failure.
