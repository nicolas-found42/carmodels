# Dealership teardown and improvements — 2026-10-12

This executed teardown targets the supplied dealership at `6c9cc06a068f2d276bd41820f15eb9922af76e7a`, including the existing GT4 branch work. It improves finding cars and recovering from mistakes without guessing undecoded identities or replacing the renderer. The task branch is `bugfix/dealership-forgiving-browsing`. Changes are local; no issue, PR, deployment or message to others was published.

The simulated persona has very little technical knowledge, misses instructions, types familiar name/year combinations, presses Enter and expects obvious recovery. This follows the user's explicit request. The observable task is to find a car, inspect it, retrieve its working GLB and recover from an empty list or failed load. A simulated reaction—“I typed the car and year; why does it say there are none?”—is a probe explanation, not participant evidence or a population preference.
