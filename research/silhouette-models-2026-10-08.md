# Source-derived 3D silhouette replacement — 2026-10-08

The gallery's 35 handling-rating box/extrusion models were replaced with car-specific display meshes baked from the recovered first-tree low-speed GLB scenes. The change is local on the existing working branch; no commit, publication or merge was requested. Existing inspector/export work was preserved.

## Geometry and display

`tools/build_silhouettes.py` verifies each GLB against its canonical recovered manifest pin and car identity, then traverses the explicit low-speed scene. It retains original translations and triangle/normal data, omits source-named driver helmets and baked `UNDER_SHADOW` planes, and welds identical position/normal/tone tuples. It does not decimate geometry or stretch it with handling ratings. The coordinate conversion is a proper rotation: source XYZ becomes gallery Z,Y,-X. Each displayed assembly is centred laterally/longitudinally and translated onto the floor.

The 35 bakes contain 152,328 triangles, ranging from 3,233 to 5,617 per car, and 161,190 stored vertices. Each car has three merged meshes and four source wheel hubs. Data is embedded in `viewer/public/cars.json`, which now occupies 6,723,675 bytes. That larger initial local payload buys synchronous car switching without runtime GLB/texture requests. All existing non-silhouette metadata, icon samples, classifications and record order compare equal to the pre-replacement data.

Source-texture vertex tones retain approximate dark/light features while suppressing strongly coloured texture regions. The icon sample supplies an illustrative body tint. These are presentation heuristics: no semantic glass/paint segmentation, decal-removal guarantee, original livery fidelity, or original shader fidelity is claimed. Source triangle, normal and UV interpretations remain candidates, and full game-render equivalence remains open. Animated/moving wheel states are outside this static gallery's scope.

The first visual bake showed self-shadow striping on thin surfaces. A normal offset of 0.035 and shadow bias of -0.00015 removed the artifact without moving model vertices. Manual image review of the [35-car contact sheet](evidence/silhouette-models-2026-10-08/contact-sheet.png) found distinct car contours, complete assemblies, and no corresponding obvious striping in the final reset-view captures. That is host visual review, not Jev pixel verification.
