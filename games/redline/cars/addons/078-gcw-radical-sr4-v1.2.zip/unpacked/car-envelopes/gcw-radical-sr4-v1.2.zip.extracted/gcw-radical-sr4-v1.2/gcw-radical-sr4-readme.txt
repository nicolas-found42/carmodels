Version:
1.2

More tweaks to the handling, new wheels and a new sound.

Handling - the brakes have been rebalanced and the braking power reduced again to make this easier to handle. The wheels are a bit lighter and the down-force a tad weightier. Net effect - slightly faster and easier to drive.

Version:
1.1

This version tweaks the handling a little. Mostly by reducing the braking power considerably so that the brakes don't lock as easily. At speeds above about 75 Mph you can use full braking, under that you may need to lift off the brake pedal as the down-force pushing the tyres into the road decreases.

On DANDY's advice I also lowered the rear sway-bar setting slightly.

Description:
An interpretation of a Radical SR4 Clubsport (2006 spec.)

Launched in 2004, the Radical SR4 Clubsport is the Uk’s most successful multi-purpose sports racing car, trackday car and extreme road car. 

The ground-breaking styling of the SR4 was inspired by the Le Mans-winning Bentley, designed by Peter Elleray, who is also responsible for overseeing Radical’s SR9 LMP2 Le Mans prototype. 

The 2006 specification SR4 has car with world class handling characteristics. The combination of a new aerodynamic package and Radical’s unique ‘Nik’ suspension system makes the SR4 not only extremely fast, but easy and forgiving to drive. 

This SR4 is fitted with a fuel-injected Powertec Suzuki 1300cc 205bhp engine, breathing through a carbon-composite ram air box
