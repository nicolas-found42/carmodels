Description:
An interpretation of a Smart Roadster Brabus and Smart Roadster Coupé Brabus V6 special.

The Smart Roadster is the rag-top sports version of the original Smart car from Daimler-Chrysler. The Brabus Roadster has a three-cylinder 698cc 101bhp turbocharged engine, the V6 has two of these amazing engines joined together in a configuration described as a 1396cc, bi-turbo engine giving it nearly 70bhp more at the cost of the already limited luggage space.

This was my first attempt at modelling a car you can buy on your high-street. I took my usual box-modelling approach and achieved a pretty good model in just a few days. Then I went the extra mile and modelled the interior, the sports seats and the wheels.

There are a few glitches: I struggled with the rear diffuser somewhat, and the body line near the door sills isn't quite right either. Rather than complicate the model by making the rear windows transparent - I chose to base this on the custom v6 model where that space is taken up with the other half of the bi-turbo engine.

Version 1.2:

Both cars now get the same version number, new badge art, and the seat textures are properly included this time.

Version 1.1:

The standard roadster is also included now, this time with a rear window. The V6's model has been tweaked with a smarter rear diffuser, rear view mirror and new textures. Both share a revised custom wheel design closer to the 3 spoke alloy offered by Smart. The handling for both have been revised from the original, which had dampers too stiff to give sensible road holding.
